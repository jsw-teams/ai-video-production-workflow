TITLE: Could You Just…
FORM: 94 bars in 4/4. Verse lines are short; chorus bars use two clipped rhythmic cells.
VOCAL: One dry, conversational lead. Let the manager repeat the hook in Chorus 2. The agents use short callouts. Keep it natural and intelligible.

[Cold Open — 2 bars]

Done.
Actually—
